Privacy and terms

CityCenter is a public demo built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. It is not run by any city, and nothing submitted here goes to a real government office. Trust City is a stand-in name.

Terms of use

  • You must be 18 or older to use this demo. It is not made for anyone under 18.
  • The desk characters are AI assistants. They can be wrong. Nothing here is legal advice, and nothing here is a decision by a government.
  • Please use made-up names and details. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.
  • You must follow the law and Meta's Model API terms, including its Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Do not use this demo from a place listed as restricted in Meta's Geographic Use Policy.
  • The demo may change, lose data or go offline at any time, with no warranty.

What this demo collects

  • What you give it: your messages, any drawing you attach, any name, phone number or email you type, and your signature.
  • Your voice: when you hold the microphone, the recording is sent for transcription. The text is kept in the box for you to send; the recording is not saved here.
  • Your internet address: used briefly to limit how many requests one connection can make. It is not saved with your visit or your signature. Our hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs.
  • Your browser: stores your zoom and voice settings on your own device. There is no advertising and no tracking.

How it is used and shared

  • Everything you submit is visible to anyone who opens the staff workspace, because that workspace is open so judges can try it. Your visit code is not shown there.
  • Your text, drawings and voice are sent to Meta's Model API to produce the replies, using Meta's Standard Services. Meta's terms say content sent to those services is not used to train its models.
  • The demo is hosted on Render. Calls to Meta may pass through a Cloudflare service that forwards them.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising or marketing.
  • Visits stay until the demo data is cleared. The demo is planned to shut down after the hackathon ends in November 2026, and its data will be deleted then.

To ask for a visit to be removed, open an issue on the project's GitHub page with its reference number only. Do not post your visit code.
