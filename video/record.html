Record the narration for the citycenter video

Pick a line on the left. Press Record (or the space bar), read the words out loud, then press Stop. Listen back and redo it if you like. Lines you have not recorded use the computer voice.

    Tips: sit close to the microphone in a quiet room. Speak a little slower than normal and smile. A short pause before and after is fine; it is trimmed automatically. Space bar starts and stops recording.
